What is the Giving Tree?

Each tag on the tree is a real need from someone in our community this Christmas. Pick one, fill it, and we’ll take it off the tree.

  1. 1

    Pick a tag.

    Tap one to see what’s needed.

  2. 2

    Fill it.

    Give online, or bring it in yourself.

  3. 3

    We’ll do the rest.

    We’ll mark it off the tree for you.

Submissions are open until December 6. Submit for yourself or someone you know.
